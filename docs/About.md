# About Loeito

**Loeito — PDF, just right.** 

**Loeito** is made in one spirit: not too little, not too much — just right. Loeito does every everyday PDF task without fuss: reading, merging, editing, signing, forms, tables, maps, OCR and safe sharing. It is designed to be calm and obvious, yet complete enough for professional work.

It follows three ideas:

- **Swedish craftsmanship:** finished, complete and durable.
- **The Swiss army knife:** one compact tool with many blades.
- **The clarity of Apple's own apps:** the document comes first.

**Private by design.** Loeito runs entirely on your computer. Files are never uploaded; the small local helper only answers Loeito itself.
