# Tutorial · Make a fillable form

_About 3 minutes._

1. Choose **Forms → Start from a template…**, pick **Leave Application**, type your organization's name and click **Create form**.
2. Click the *Reason* field and set it **Required**.
3. Select three fields (drag a box around them). Under **Arrange**, choose *Selection* and click **Align left**, then **Distribute vertically**.
4. Select a field and click **Show only when…**: show it only when *Type of leave* is *Other*.
5. Select the *Reason* field and click **Add label**: type *Why do you need this leave?*, make it bold and blue. Drag the field: the label comes along, with a red * because the field is Required.
6. Under **Add text & shapes**, pick **Box** and drag a box around the dates. Choose a light fill.
7. Click **Fill in** and try it: totals update and conditional fields appear.
8. Save, choosing *Keep fillable*.

To convert an existing form: open the Word, Excel or scanned form and click **Find fields**.
