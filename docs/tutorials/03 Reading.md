# Tutorial · Reading and focus mode

_About 1 minute._

1. Open a long PDF and choose **Read**.
2. Scroll. Use **Ctrl-wheel** to zoom; click the **%** to change the zoom speed or pick Fit width.
3. Choose a theme (Paper, Sepia, Night) and one or two pages side by side.
4. Select some text. The small bar offers **Copy, Highlight, Find, Speak**.
5. Press **F** for focus mode: only the page, a progress line and the reading time left. **Esc** leaves.
6. Click **Play** under *Listen* to have the document read aloud.
