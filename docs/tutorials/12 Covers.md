# Tutorial · Add a book cover

_About 2 minutes._

1. Choose **Covers**, keep **Front cover** selected.
2. Type the title, subtitle and author. Open *More details* for the publisher, edition or supervisor.
3. Click designs in the gallery (Classic, Swiss, Thesis…) and watch the preview change. Pick an accent color.
4. Optional: **Add picture or logo**.
5. Click **Add as first page**.
6. Switch to **Last page**, choose *Back cover* or *Colophon*, type a short description, and click **Add as last page**.
