# Loeito roadmap

Loeito aims to be the calm, complete, private PDF workbench: every everyday task, just right. The directions below are broad intentions, not promises or dates.

## Now

- Polishing what is there: clearer wording, smoother motion, fewer clicks, better results on difficult scans.
- Desktop installers for Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Next

- **Review & print:** compare two versions of a document; check a file before it goes to print; keep bookmarks when saving.
- **Accessible PDFs:** check and repair documents so screen readers can read them.
- **Urdu, Arabic and Persian, first class:** every tool working right to left with joined letters, plus vertical Chinese and Japanese. [The plan](docs/plans/Urdu,%20Arabic,%20Persian%20and%20vertical%20scripts.md).
- **Better OCR, your choice of engine:** a more accurate engine (PaddleOCR) next to today's, a score that shows whether your computer can run each one, comparing both, and bringing your own model. [The plan](docs/plans/Better%20OCR.md).
- **More languages:** Loeito's menus in more languages.
- **Professional print management, made visual:** folded booklets and books printed in the right order, right way up, with a 3D preview and a guide for your own printer; several pages per sheet, cards, posters, print-shop marks and checks before printing. [The plan](docs/plans/Print%20and%20booklets.md).
- **Page cleanup:** find blank and repeated pages and remove them after you confirm.
- **Repair and archive:** fix damaged PDFs, make long-term PDF/A files, and prepare files for fast web viewing.
- **Scan cleanup:** straighten, brighten and clean scans, or turn pages gray or negative, before OCR.
- **Compare two versions:** see exactly what changed, in text and appearance, even after re-export from Word or when one is a scan; save a report. [The plan](docs/plans/Compare,%20Preferences%20and%20Measure.md).
- **Preferences:** one place to set your defaults for every tool.
- **Measure:** calibrate to any scale, then measure length, area and angles, with fine adjustment.
- **More formats:** EPUB in and out, Markdown in and out, and PDF to editable Word.
- **Smoother working:** a Before/After switch on every tool that changes pages, recent files you can send to any tool, and a command finder that understands plain requests such as "remove footer".

## Later

- **Repeatable work:** save a sequence of steps and run it over many files.
- **Trust:** sign with certificates and verify signatures.
- **Richer documents:** attachments, layers and portfolios.
- **Smart batch:** one recipe over a folder: name files from their contents (invoice number, date), clean, OCR and stamp, with a preview before anything changes.
- **Document health check:** one report on a file (damaged parts, hidden data, unreadable text, oversized images, print and archive problems), each with a Fix button.
- **Safe to share:** one click that finds private details, hidden data and metadata, shows them, then removes them and compares the result.
- **Forms from a scan:** turn a scanned paper form into a fillable one, with Urdu, Arabic and Persian labels.
- **Picture layouts:** place several pictures or pages on a sheet with templates, shared with Covers.

## Always

- Private by design: files never leave your computer.
- Free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3.
- Friction-free workflow: one task, many paths, by mouse or by keyboard. Still growing, and each new version is one more step toward it.
- Simple first, powerful when you need it.

Ideas and feedback are welcome. Open an issue on GitHub.
