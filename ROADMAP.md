# Loeito roadmap

Loeito aims to be the calm, complete, private PDF workbench: every everyday task, just right. The directions below are broad intentions, not promises or dates.

## Now

- Polishing what is there: clearer wording, smoother motion, fewer clicks, better results on difficult scans.
- Desktop installers for Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Next

- **Review & print:** compare two versions of a document; check a file before it goes to print; keep bookmarks when saving.
- **Accessible PDFs:** check and repair documents so screen readers can read them.
- **Urdu, Arabic and Persian, first class:** every tool working right to left with joined letters, plus vertical Chinese and Japanese. [The plan](docs/plans/Urdu,%20Arabic,%20Persian%20and%20vertical%20scripts.md).
- **Better OCR, your choice of engine:** a more accurate engine (PaddleOCR) next to today's, a score that shows whether your computer can run each one, comparing both, and bringing your own model. [The plan](docs/plans/Better%20OCR.md).
- **More languages:** Loeito's menus in more languages.

## Later

- **Repeatable work:** save a sequence of steps and run it over many files.
- **Trust:** sign with certificates and verify signatures.
- **Richer documents:** attachments, layers and portfolios.

## Always

- Private by design: files never leave your computer.
- Free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3.
- Simple first, powerful when you need it.

Ideas and feedback are welcome. Open an issue on GitHub.
